Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where people could enjoy carefully made food and connect with their community.

What started as a small local bakery has grown into a place where families, commuters, and event planners can find fresh breads, pastries, and cakes made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking begins with good ingredients. We seek quality ingredients and work with local and regional suppliers whenever practical. We choose ingredients with attention to freshness, quality, and responsible sourcing.
